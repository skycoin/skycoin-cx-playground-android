This app holds no program. It is a thin programnetwork node: it connects
to the node its args give, downloads the program at the manifest address
its args give, checks the signature and every block hash, and is done,
with no subscription and nothing kept. Then it runs the program, at once
when a trusted key of its args signed it and otherwise once the user
confirms it.
See "Node types (#34, #44)" in docs/ProgramDistribution.md.
