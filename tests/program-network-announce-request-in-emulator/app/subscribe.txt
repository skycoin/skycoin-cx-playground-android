This app holds no program. It runs a programnetwork node and subscribes to
the nodes its args give. Every have they send goes in its have queue, it
reads each one, and its policy requests the program from a node that
announced it. It checks the signature and every block hash, then runs
the program, at once when a trusted key of its args signed it and
otherwise once the user confirms it.
See "Announce and request" in docs/ProgramDistribution.md.
